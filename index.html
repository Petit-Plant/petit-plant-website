Our Story

A new way of growing, born in Ouarzazate.

Petit Plant began with a simple fascination: discovering that plants could thrive without soil, provided they had the right conditions, care and nutrients.

Years later, that fascination became a vision. In Ouarzazate, a remarkable region where growing fresh produce comes with its own challenges, Petit Plant brings together modern hydroponic agriculture, careful cultivation and a deep appreciation for fresh, quality produce.
